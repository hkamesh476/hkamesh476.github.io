Bank of Begonia

I started a carnival for my community, and then built the ticket system it runs on. Bank of Begonia is a small bank for carnival tickets: every visitor has a QR wallet, stalls scan it to charge tickets, friends can send tickets to each other, and a central server keeps every balance straight.

Organizers and stall owners see live numbers for every stall, so they know where the crowd is and when to restock.

It's open to everyone. You can sign up for your own wallet today.

    My Résumé

    I'm interested in mathematics and economics, and I'm looking for a chance to help with research and development on projects with real-world impact.

    1. MathCORP

      Teaching assistant, winner of the Excellence in Teaching Award

      Guided 8th graders through Algebra II and introduced foundational precalculus. Wrote and handed out my own geometry enrichment lessons on SOHCAHTOA and special right triangles. Ran a classroom that put intuition and confidence ahead of memorizing.

    2. Bank of Begonia

      Lead product developer

      A ticket-based mini bank for carnivals and events, open for anyone to sign up. QR codes and a central server let visitors receive, spend and transfer digital tickets securely, and give organizers and stall owners live statistics.
