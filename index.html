FounderI started it

Bank of Begonia

I started a carnival for my community, and then built the ticket system it runs on. Bank of Begonia is a small bank for carnival tickets: every visitor has a QR wallet, stalls scan it to charge tickets, friends can send tickets to each other, and a central server keeps every balance straight.

Organizers and stall owners see live numbers for every stall, so they know where the crowd is and when to restock.

Version 2 is called Carnival Cash, and it's open to everyone. You can sign up for your own wallet at cc.teabreaktech.com.
